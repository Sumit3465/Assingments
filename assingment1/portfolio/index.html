About Me

/ 01

I'm a Full Stack Developer who enjoys working across the entire stack — designing clean, responsive interfaces on one end, and building reliable, well-structured APIs and databases on the other. What draws me to development is the problem-solving: taking a messy, real-world requirement and shaping it into software that actually works for people.

I care about writing code that's readable and maintainable, not just code that runs. Whether it's a small utility or a full application, I like understanding the "why" behind a feature before I build the "how".

Outside of shipping features, I'm usually learning a new tool, sharpening my fundamentals, or picking apart a tricky algorithm for fun.
